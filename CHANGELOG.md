# Changelog

Versions follow `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`. The installed version is shown next to the title of the interface
and in **Tools > Updates**, which also tells when a newer version is published.

## 0.9.0 — 2026-11-18

First numbered version. Everything below the training engine is experimental: tested on an RTX 3090
under Windows 11 for training, and on simulated data for the measurement and dataset tools.

### Training
- **8 GB preset** (experimental) for 8 GB cards such as the RTX 2000 Ada or the A2000: w4a8 base, 90 %
  of the transformer offloaded to RAM (up to 95 %), 512 px, 1024 px previews. About 40 GB of RAM at
  peak, 64 GB recommended; much slower than on a 24 GB card.
- **Several captions per image**: a `.txt` with several lines gives one variant per line; each variant
  is trained with the same image (same cached latents). Automatic captioning can write 2 or 3 variants.
- **Repeats per subfolder**: a subfolder named `3_back` is seen three times per pass over the dataset.
  Subfolders are now handled everywhere (audit, caption fixer, cropping, likeness, reports).
- Automagic safeguards: switch to AdamW 8-bit at 2e-4 when regularization images are used, warning
  with DOP, learning rate shown next to the speed and an alert when it collapses below 1e-6.
- Against bleeding: DOP preservation and regularization images generated by the base model.
- Power sweep at the start of training, thermostat, thermal pause, GPU power limit.
- Fast 512 px and full Fizgig recipe processes; pause with full state, queue, scheduled pause.

### Choosing checkpoints
- Likeness (DINOv2) and prompt fidelity (CLIP) of every preview, recommended checkpoint.
- Bleeding detection with an automatic control prompt, alert and optional pause.
- Warning when preview prompts lack the trigger word or contain a near variant of it.
- Loss chart (smoothed), power / speed / temperature chart with a recommended power threshold.

### Dataset
- Audit: duplicates, near-identical series, plain backgrounds, image sizes.
- Caption fixer for character LoRAs, with backup and restore.
- Automatic captioning (Qwen3-VL) adapted to the LoRA type; cropping of watermarks and text.

### Interface
- Seven languages (interface, `.bat` scripts, manual); electricity cost; per-tab logs and progress;
  the running job comes back after a page refresh or a language change.
- Update check for the tool (GitHub Releases) and for ai-toolkit.
