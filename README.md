# H3-Image LoRA Portable

A portable Windows installer and a Python graphical interface (Gradio) to train **MiniMax-H3-Image**
LoRAs with [ostris/ai-toolkit](https://github.com/ostris/ai-toolkit), tuned for an
**RTX 3090 24 GB** and **64 GB of RAM**. No Node.js or npm: ai-toolkit's official Next.js interface
is replaced by a Gradio interface.

> **Languages:** the interface, the `.bat` scripts and the manual are available in English (default),
> French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese and Mandarin. Manuals are in [manuals/](manuals/)
> ([English](manuals/en_README.txt)).

> **Status: experimental.** Training has been validated only on an RTX 3090 under Windows 11, with
> the "Standard" process (int8 base, 35 % of the layers offloaded to RAM). The likeness and bleeding
> measurements, the dataset audit, the caption fixer, the power sweep and the anti-bleeding
> protections have been tested on simulated data, not yet on real training runs; their thresholds
> may need tuning. Options marked "experimental" in the interface have not been verified on real
> hardware. Feedback is welcome in the *Issues*.

## Why this tool

ai-toolkit is an excellent training engine. This project does not replace it: it drives it, and adds
what is usually done by hand, by eye or with separate tools — choosing the right checkpoint,
cleaning the dataset, keeping a 24 GB card cool and efficient over a night of training.

**Pick the right checkpoint with numbers, not only by eye**
- Each preview is scored for **likeness** to the dataset (DINOv2) and **prompt fidelity** (CLIP),
  in the spirit of the DINO and CLIP-T metrics of the DreamBooth paper. The interface recommends the most faithful checkpoint
  that still follows the prompt as well as the base model, instead of the last one.
- **Bleeding detection**: a control prompt without the trigger word (added automatically if missing)
  shows when the character starts to appear everywhere. Strong bleeding sends a notification and can
  pause the training; those checkpoints are excluded from the recommendation.
- A warning when the preview prompts lack the trigger word or contain a near variant of it (a
  frequent cause of "the LoRA does nothing").

**Fix the dataset before it costs a night of training**
- **Dataset audit**: duplicates, series of near-identical images and uniform backgrounds that the
  LoRA would learn as part of the character; image sizes are checked against the training
  resolutions.
- **Caption fixer**: for a character LoRA, proposes removing the fixed traits (hair, eyes, outfit...)
  from the captions so that they are carried by the trigger word, with a review table, a backup and
  a one-click restore.
- **Several captions per image** (one per line of the `.txt`), so the LoRA clings less to one wording;
  automatic captioning (Qwen3-VL), adapted to the LoRA type, can write 2 or 3 variants.
- **Repeats per subfolder**: a subfolder named `3_back` is seen three times per pass, to give weight to
  rare views without duplicating files.
- Automatic cropping of watermarks and text.

**Against bleeding**
- One-click **DOP preservation** (ai-toolkit's differential output preservation).
- **Regularization images** generated by the base model from your own captions (trigger word replaced
  by the class), stored next to the dataset and mixed into the training.

**Use the hardware well, and know what it costs**
- **Power sweep**: at the start of a training run, tries decreasing GPU power limits and keeps the
  lowest one that stays within 3 % of full speed. A chart of power, speed and temperature recommends
  a threshold from your own runs.
- Thermostat (adjusts the power limit to hold a target temperature), thermal alert and pause, GPU
  power limit during jobs, sleep prevention.
- **Electricity cost** of the GPU, spent so far and projected at the end of the job, in your currency.
- A 20-step **quick test** that measures speed and memory, estimates the total time and suggests the
  RAM offload ratio.

**Long runs without babysitting**
- Pause with full state saving and resume, job queue, scheduled pause (at a step or a time).
- One log and one progress panel per tab (captioning, training, tests, downloads), with an immediate
  response and an ETA.
- The page can be refreshed, or its language changed, during a training run: the running job's
  settings and previews come back.
- Notifications by sound and on the phone (ntfy.sh).

**Portable and multilingual**
- Everything is downloaded into the folder (MinGit, uv, Python 3.12, torch, FFmpeg); nothing is
  installed system-wide, no Node.js. Downloads show progress and resume after interruptions.
- Seven languages for the interface, the `.bat` scripts and the manual.
- Version shown in the interface, with a check for new releases of the tool and of ai-toolkit
  ([CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md)).

## Training processes

- **Standard ai-toolkit**, with 24 GB presets.
- **Fast 512 px**: settings from the Fizgig recipe on ai-toolkit components.
- **Full Fizgig recipe** (experimental): Fizgig training adapter, likeness optimization, LCM previews.

Checkpoints can be compared side by side (same prompts, same seed) and copied to ComfyUI.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, a 24 GB NVIDIA GPU (recent driver: 580 series or later for CUDA 13).
  8 GB cards (RTX 2000 Ada, A2000...) can try the experimental **8 GB preset**: w4a8 base, 90 % of
  the transformer in RAM, 512 px, about 40 GB of RAM at peak, and much slower steps.
- 64 GB of RAM recommended, 80 GB of disk space (environment + model weights).
- A folder path **without spaces**, for example `D:\AI\H3-LoRA\`.
- Administrator rights only for the GPU power limit, the thermostat and the power sweep
  (`2_LANCER_UI_ADMIN.bat`).

## Installation

1. Download the repository: **Code → Download ZIP**, or the latest version under **Releases**.
2. Unzip it into a folder whose path contains no spaces.
3. Run `1_INSTALLER.bat` (10 to 30 minutes; it can be restarted at any time and resumes where it stopped).
4. Run `2_LANCER_UI.bat`: the interface opens at http://127.0.0.1:7860.

Updating: unzip the new version over the old one; a running training job keeps going, and a page
refresh picks up the new interface.

## Measurements on an RTX 3090

Author's measurements, from the *quick test* and real training runs, at 1024 + 1536 resolution:

| Setting | Speed | Peak VRAM |
|---|---|---|
| int8 base, 35 % in RAM | 4.0 to 6.5 s per step | 19.6 to 20.6 GB |
| w4a8 base | 15.2 s per step | 19.0 GB |

The "fast 512 px" process ran at about 2.5 s per step on the same card. GPU temperature has a large
effect on speed: an RTX 3090 throttles above 83 °C.
